Privacy Policy for MJH Studio

Developer Information

MJH Studio is developed by MJH and hosted on Netlify. For privacy questions, contact: your-email@example.com

No personal data collection: This app does not collect, store, or share any personal information such as name, email, location, or device identifiers.

The app uses browser localStorage to save user projects (HTML/Blockly code) locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device or gets sent to any server.

CDNs: Blockly, FontAwesome, icons load from CDN but no personal data shared.
